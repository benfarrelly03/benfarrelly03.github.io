Concept

Twinship as a spectrum of identity, where similarity and difference move between alignment, gentle variation and dramatic change.

Patterned fabrics sit alongside contrast knit. Stripes, paired silhouettes and repeated shapes reference the outside expectation that twins should match, while breaks in colour, texture and construction mark the moments where identity begins to separate.

The line up is structured around four identity statuses, Diffusion, Foreclosure, Moratorium and Achievement, and moves from a shared boyhood towards more defined personal expression, blending masculine and feminine references along the way.

Using a fine gauge knit machine with merino and Grilon thermoplastic yarns, I set out to create rigidity in a medium that is usually soft, using knit to control the narrative of twinship through silhouette and fabrication.

Technical

Construction
Rugby knit
Fully fashioned raglan, knitted on a 12 gauge industrial Dubied and linked on a 12 gauge linker with chain stitch
Ribs
Neck and arm ribs doubled and sandwiched, six rows on the front bed of a Passap 8 gauge
Yarns
100% merino, Expotex Grilon K85, 100% cotton ribs
Peplum jacket
100% bonded wool with silk lining, knitted merino and Grilon pleats, pleated cap sleeves, blue topstitching and cream buttonholes
