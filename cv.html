Profile

I am a recent National College of Art and Design fashion design graduate specialising in knitwear, with interests in colour theory, silhouette and experimental textiles. Learning through making is central to my practice. I am seeking my first design role, bringing practical skills and a commitment to developing my work in fashion and craft.
