About

Knitwear and textile designer from Kildare, working from yarn, gauge and structure up.

I trained in Fashion Design (Knit) at the National College of Art and Design in Dublin, graduating with First Class Honours in 2026. My work is driven by colour, silhouette and experimental textiles, and by the process of making as much as the finished piece. For my graduate collection I used a fine gauge machine with merino and Grilon thermoplastic yarns to create rigidity in a medium that is usually soft.
